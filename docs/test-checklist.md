# Divebomb test checklist

Tick each one. Anything odd, write a line in the testing thread with the model, setting and what you heard.

## Treadle feel (expression pedal)

- [ ] Calibrate on the PEDAL page. Heel is the original pitch, toe is the setting's full interval
- [ ] FIVE, 1 OCT UP: a slow sweep is smooth with no steps, a fast rock follows your foot with no lag
- [ ] Toe all the way down lands on a true octave (check against an open string an octave up)
- [ ] Heel all the way down is fully dry
- [ ] DIVE BOMB: a slow heel to toe on a held note dives three octaves smoothly
- [ ] Drag the on-screen treadle: Pebble stretches as it rises, squashes as it dives

## Latency

- [ ] Live, Classic, guitar: play fast single-note lines with 1 OCT UP at toe. Feels immediate
- [ ] Live, Classic, bass with Input on BASS: low notes feel tight, with the dry low end under the shift
- [ ] Note the figure on the LATENCY card as you play high and low notes
- [ ] Studio: chords sound smoother, and Ableton keeps the track in time when you record
- [ ] Try Ableton at 64 and 128 samples and note which you'd use live

## Bass tracking (in every model)

- [ ] FIVE, DUAL, NEON and BASS, 1 OCT UP at toe: open B (or E) and a few frets up stay in tune, no wobble
- [ ] Let a low note ring out: it keeps its pitch as it fades
- [ ] Low Anchor on (AUTO with Input BASS): the bottom never drops out on bends, drops and dive bombs
- [ ] Move CROSSOVER: lower keeps more of the shift, higher keeps more punch
- [ ] Slap or pick fast sixteenths: notes don't smear into each other

## Treadle section

- [ ] Each model's settings match the dial (21 positions)
- [ ] Harmony settings keep the dry note and bend the second note between the two intervals
- [ ] NEON Octave settings morph between the two octave blends
- [ ] Detune adds the thick copy as you go toe down, and Pebble wobbles
- [ ] Classic on chords warbles like the original, Chords is smooth
- [ ] GLITCH: 0 is clean, 1 the original, 2 wilder
- [ ] DUAL shift layer: drop and raise, octave plus dry, used together with a bend
- [ ] Layer GLIDE at INSTANT is an instant switch, longer glides smoothly

## Stomp section

- [ ] The drop: each setting down to octave plus dry, momentary and latch
- [ ] Latch the drop on, switch to MOMENTARY: D works as a kill switch
- [ ] HAMMER on B: instant jumps that land on the beat, back on release
- [ ] C in HAMMER, IMPOSSIBLE (alternates P2 and 2ND), SEQ 2, 3 and 5 (steps, Pitch 1 hammers relative to the step)
- [ ] TRILL with SYNC: rapid jumps in time with Ableton. TAP sets a free rate
- [ ] Sequence with Trill: ONCE, AGAIN and LOOP behave as named
- [ ] DRY + adds the dry note under jumps
- [ ] GLIDE on B: SHIFT and RETURN times, up and down, octave plus dry, momentary and latch. The ladder runs with the glide and cycles when idle
- [ ] ORIGINAL TONE rolls off the top in Chords, as the compact pedals do

## Footswitches

- [ ] Open Divebomb with nothing set up: FOOTSWITCHES READY appears, A to D work straight away
- [ ] Treadle: A on/off, hold A Classic/Chords. B momentary whammy (RISE and FALL). C next, hold C back. D tap latches the layer, hold is momentary
- [ ] Stomp: A on/off, hold A Trill. B Pitch 1 or glide. C Pitch 2. D drop
- [ ] A+B switches section with no stray bypass
- [ ] Hold B+C one second: model steps FIVE, DUAL, NEON, BASS with no stray jump
- [ ] Hold C+D one second: KEPT, Pebble nods, no stray jump or drop
- [ ] Two copies of Divebomb: tap C+D to hand the footswitches over
- [ ] The on-screen A to D buttons do exactly the same as the real ones

## Neon skin

- [ ] Choose NEON: the panel flips tile by tile, left to right, to neon yellow and orange with gaffer tape
- [ ] Pebble turns orange with a strip of tape
- [ ] Every label is readable on yellow, the flip cards stay black with white digits
- [ ] Choose any other model: it flips back to the dark look
- [ ] Switch to NEON with B+C held: the wipe still runs

## Keeper

- [ ] KEEP button and hold C+D save an entry with 8 seconds of audio in Documents, Flip Fella Keepers
- [ ] The note field appears for ten seconds and never blocks playing
- [ ] Right-click KEEP: Export for Claude makes a zip
